| Threshold | Hit Rate | Refusal Correctness |
|---|---|---|
| 0.50 | 95% | 0% |
| 0.55 | 95% | 30% |

| 0.70 | 80% | 100% |
| 0.75 (old) | 55% | 100% |

### Analytical Justification for `RETRIEVAL_SCORE_THRESHOLD = 0.65`

1. **Pareto-optimal on measured data**: highest threshold keeping full 95% ranking accuracy while refusing 100% of negatives (max negative 0.6396 → cushion +0.0104; min kept positive 0.6697).
2. **Fail-safe miss**: the single sub-threshold answerable (`INC-BENCH-009`, 0.5574) ranked the *wrong* article — gate converts it into a clean refusal.
3. **Old 0.75 retired**: it rejected 9 correctly-ranked articles (scores 0.67–0.75, incl. 4/5 paraphrases) — a 55% hit rate that would refuse nearly half of genuine traffic.
